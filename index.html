Services

One workshop, every process.

Cutting, edging, CNC machining and spray finishing in the Brookhouse factory — no handing your job between subcontractors, no waiting between stages.

Cut & edge

Boards cut to size on our beam saw and edged with a seamless, durable finish. Ideal for cabinet carcasses, wardrobes, shelving and replacement panels.

  • Cut to size in MFC, MDF, plywood and more
  • Edge banding in matching or contrast finishes
  • Optimised nesting to cut waste and cost
Find out moreShop — build your cutting list

CNC machining

Full 3-axis routing, drilling, pocketing, v-carving and profile cutting for cabinet joinery, signage, templates and one-off pieces — from your CAD files or ours.

  • Works from AutoCAD, Corel Draw and other vector files
  • Edge drilling and slotting for cabinet assembly
  • Wide range of sheet materials machined in-house
Find out moreShop — upload a CNC job

Spray finishing

Once your panels are cut, machined and edged, our spray shop applies the colour coat or protective finish — matched to the shade you need, applied in factory conditions.

  • Primed and painted panels ready to fit
  • Colour matching to your sample or reference
  • Durable finishes for kitchens and high-wear joinery
